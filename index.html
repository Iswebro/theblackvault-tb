Make difficulty readable, not arbitrary.

Narrative, card, enemy and relic language is designed alongside guided playtest prompts so the game can explain pressure, reward smart play and turn feedback into the next design pass.

See the editorial principle

Punishing but fair. Readable danger. Wins should feel earned.

Enemy identity should come from distinctive patterns, telegraphs and personality, not merely larger numbers. Feedback copy must also accept partial and defeat runs as useful evidence rather than treating them as failed submissions.

AI operationsProduct governanceQA

A multi-model system with a human at the center.

Nexus work uses source-grounded prompts, lane-specific roles, independent challenge, tests and pull-request gates. Model output is treated as a claim to verify, never a source of truth.

See the operating model
  • Build a source pack from the product, repository and real user questions.
  • Assign a clear lane: research, copy, architecture, implementation or review.
  • Challenge unsupported claims with evidence.
  • Run connected-surface QA, not only a happy-path check.
  • Keep final product and editorial approval human-owned.

AI creative operating system

Fast does not mean careless.

AI can widen the option space. Evidence, challenge and human editorial judgment decide what gets published.

Build the source pack

Product truth, audience questions, live state, channel, constraints and forbidden claims.

Generate with intent

Angles, outlines, objections, compression, variants and useful disagreement.

Challenge the draft

Evidence, likely misreading, compliance, support burden and reputation risk.

Human edit

Voice, rhythm, specificity and clarity. Remove generic language and inflated certainty.

Approve every word

No model is a source of truth. Every claim remains human-owned.

Learn from users

Use questions, feedback and performance to improve the next brief.
